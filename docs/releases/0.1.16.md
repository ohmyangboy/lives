# Lives 0.1.16

发布日期：2026-10-06

加入胶卷关键帧控制与 1–15 秒时长调节，并统一品牌图标、补充 Lives Mobile 入口。

- 新增胶卷与观片器关键帧控制，支持拖动、Shift 精细调节及切换画格时的收卷与展开过渡。
- 拖动时间线两侧手柄，将 Live 时长调节为 1–15 秒，精度为 0.1 秒；所有画格同步采用所选时长。
- 时长调节时暂停预览，播放结束后柔和回到所选关键帧。
- 导出保留所选片段起点，短素材延长末帧补齐，避免长时长输出出现黑色尾帧。
- Mac 应用和标题栏改用官网的黑金图标。
- 反馈面板新增 Lives Mobile 图标、App Store 下载二维码、“已上架 App Store，欢迎使用”提示及官网入口。

支持 Apple Silicon，最低 macOS 13。下载与 SHA-256 见 [GitHub Release](https://github.com/ohmyangboy/lives/releases/tag/v0.1.16)。

---

Adds film-style keyframe controls and adjustable 1–15 second duration, alongside refreshed branding and Lives Mobile links.

- Adds film cartridge and loupe keyframe controls with dragging, Shift fine adjustment, and rewind/unroll transitions when switching cells.
- Adjusts Live Photo duration from 1 to 15 seconds in 0.1-second steps using the timeline edge handles; all cells use the selected duration.
- Pauses preview while trimming and smoothly returns to the selected keyframe after playback.
- Preserves selected segment starts and extends the final frame of short sources to avoid black tails in longer exports.
- Updates the Mac app and title bar to the official black-and-gold icon.
- Adds the Lives Mobile icon, App Store download QR code, availability message, and website link to the feedback panel.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 13 or later. Downloads and SHA-256 checksums are available on the [GitHub Release](https://github.com/ohmyangboy/lives/releases/tag/v0.1.16).
