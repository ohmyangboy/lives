# Lives 0.1.18

发布日期：2026-11-02

精简编辑与导出界面，新增快捷 Live 时长和最优画质输出。

- 顶栏采用图标与两字文字操作，导出位于最右；重置拼贴保留素材。
- 统一琥珀色选中态，简化素材项目标签、滚动条与画布工具排版。
- 时间线左上角可快捷选择 1、1.5、2、3、5、10 或 15 秒，仍支持拖动手柄精细调整。
- 导出默认选择最优画质，并保留均衡档；根据素材与裁剪计算输出尺寸，长边最多 3840 像素。
- 精简导出和成功弹窗，说明收进信息弹层，采用系统照片与文件夹图标。

---

Simplifies editing and export, with quick Live duration controls and best-quality output.

- Compact icon-and-text toolbar actions, with Export on the far right; resetting the collage keeps imported media.
- Consistent amber selections and cleaner project tabs, scrollbars, and canvas controls.
- Quickly choose 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 5, 10, or 15 seconds from the timeline, while keeping precise handle trimming.
- Best quality is selected by default alongside Balanced; output follows source media and crops, up to a 3840-pixel long edge.
- Streamlined export and success dialogs, information popovers, and system Photos and folder icons.
